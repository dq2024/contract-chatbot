MODIFICATION NO. 1 TO AGREEMENT 23145 Snow Removal Services 

This Modification to Agreement 23145 (“Modification”) is made effective upon signing by Lake County, IL (“County”) and Mark 1 Landscape ("Mark 1"), with principal offices at 1540 Hecht Dr. unit K, Bartlett, IL 60103. The County and Mark 1 are referred to as the “Parties.” 

## RECITALS 

- WHEREAS, 1. Mark 1 was awarded a contract by the County for snow removal services at County buildings which was executed on November 14, 2023. 

- 2. The Parties identified additional services necessary to be added into the scope of work. 3. The County expectations for Mark 1 to report any incidents requires clarification. 4. The Parties have agreed to amend the terms of the Agreement as set forth in this Modification. 

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the recitals above and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree that the Agreement shall be modified as follows: 

## Modification of existing sections of the Agreement: 

- Bid Item 1 in groups A-F shall be modified to read as follows: “Snow Plowing, including de-icing/salting for an event greater than 1” to 5”” 

Pricing will remain as written in the agreement for events greater than 2” up to 5” for each location. 

## Section 4. Snow Plowing 

The second sentence of this section shall be replaced with: “Snow plowing shall occur after snow accumulation greater than 1”, prior to 8:00 AM or the opening time at each facility on days with the facility is open.” 

## New sections added to the Agreement: 

## Bid Item 13, Groups A-F: On-call services 

- The services below shall be added as on-call services in groups A-F: Clearing sidewalks and parking lots, including de-icing/salting for ice or a trace amount of snow up to 1” may be requested by the County on an on-call basis only and may be site specific. 

The on-call services above shall be billed at the equivalent group rates written in the agreement for events greater than 2” up to 5”. 

- Unit Price Bid Item 15.0 Additional Related Services as Requested Should the County have additional related needs not included in the services listed and Mark 1 accepts the service request, the County agrees to pay Mark 1 at the hourly rate of $90.00/hour. 

## 15. Documentation for Hourly Fees 

Mark 1 agrees to attach detailed time tracking documentation to each invoice that contains any hourly fees and the County reserves the right to verify accuracy. The documentation shall include the date, time, description of services, number of Mark 1 employees assigned to the task, the hours worked by each employee, and any necessary details for County verification. Non-compliance may result in delayed payment or an invoice dispute. 

## 16. Incident Reporting 

In the event of any incidents, accidents, damages, injuries or issues related to the services under this contract, Mark 1 shall immediately report such incidents to the County both verbally and in writing. Communication shall be made first by phone and then followed by email. Phone calls shall be made in order listed below until Mark 1 reaches a person to speak with directly. Written notification shall be sent to all email addresses listed and include a detailed description of[the][incident,][the][date][and][time][it][occurred,][the][location,][pictures][ from][related] incident, and any additional pertinent information.
