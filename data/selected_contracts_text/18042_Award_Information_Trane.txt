## **Joint Purchase Information** 

## **Scope of Work:** 

Provide routine inspections for Trane Chillers, Air Cooled Condensing Units, and Building Automation Systems that have been installed at the following facilities: Admin Tower, Courthouse Parking Garage, Mundelein, Park City, Round Lake Branch Courts, and Emergency Telephone System Board (ETSB). 

## **Procurement Method:** 

Facilities identified a national cooperative contract with U.S. Communities Government Purchasing Alliance (15-JLP-023) that provides the cost for goods, services and labor at an already negotiated rate.  This is authorized by Article 10, Section 101 of the Lake County Purchasing Ordinance. 

## **Department Recommendation:** 

The Purchasing Division vetted the contract with the U.S. Communities and determined that it met the requirements pursuant to State of Illinois Joint Purchasing Act and the Lake County Purchasing Ordinance.  Lake County may participate in a joint purchasing agreements for the procurement of goods with one or more public procurement units in accordance with an agreement entered into between the participants.
