## **Scope of Work:** 

SimplexGrinnell will maintain, service, and annually test the fire alarms in the County ’ s facilities to ensure they are operational and code compliant. 

## **Procurement Method:** 

Facilities identified a national cooperative contract with the National Joint Powers Alliance (NJPA) that provides the cost for goods, services and labor at an already negotiated rate.  This is authorized by Article 10, Section 101 of the Lake County Purchasing Ordinance. 

## **Department Recommendation:** 

The Purchasing Division vetted the contract with NJPA and determined that it met the requirements pursuant to State of Illinois Joint Purchasing Act and the Lake County Purchasing Ordinance.  Lake County may participate in a joint purchasing agreements for the procurement of goods with one or more public procurement units in accordance with an agreement entered into between the participants.
