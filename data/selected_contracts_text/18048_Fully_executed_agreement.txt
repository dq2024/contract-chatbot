## # 18048 ONE STOP OPERATOR MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING FOR PROFESSIONAL SERVICES For LAKE COUNTY 

This MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING is entered into this _L day one? , 2018, by and between Lake County Workforce Development Board (“Workforce Development Board”), Lake County and the One-Stop Operator partner consortium between Lake County Workforce Development Department, College of Lake County, and Illinois Department of Employment Security (“OSO”), (the Workforce Development Board, Lake County, and the OSO are collectively referred to as the “parties”). 

## RECITALS 

WHEREAS, the Workforce Development Board is seeking a One-Stop Operator, as noted in RFP# 18048 documents, to provide services at the One-Stop Center in Lake County and the Job Center of Lake County and the OSO, a partner consortium between Lake County Workforce Development, College of Lake County, and Illinois Department of Employment Security, submitted a proposal dated March 8, 2018, to provide such services (“Services”) and the OSO has been selected, through a competitive bid process, to provide such Services; and WHEREAS, OSO has the professional expertise and credentials to provide these Services and has agreed to assume responsibility for providing these Services; and WHEREAS, Section 121 of the Workforce Investment and Opportunity Act, Pub. L. 113-128, “(WIOA”) requires a memorandum of understanding be entered into between the local board, with the agreement of the chief elected official, and the one-stop partner (the “MOQU”); and 

WHEREAS, the parties recognize that the Services provided hereunder are pursuant to the requirement of the WIOA and that all rules and regulations, WIOA state policies and any other state or federal requirements per WIOA will be applicable. 

NOW, THEREFORE, Workforce Development Board, with the agreement of Lake County, and the OSO agree as follows:

## SECTION 2. SCOPE OF WORK 

The OSO shall coordinate partner activities and services to ensure that the One-Stop Center in Lake County and the Job Center of Lake County continue to meet and improve upon the related One-Stop Certification criteria under the Illinois Workforce Innovation Board Criteria and Procedures for Certifying One-Stop Centers. 

The OSO is required to maintain a One-Stop Center in Lake County and the Job Center of Lake County located in Waukegan. The OSO shall have an on-site presence at the Job Center of Lake County. The Job Center Supervisor will dedicate no less than 75% of time carrying out the role as the on-site lead and as part of the leadership advisory partnership. 

Within 45 days of execution of this MOU and subsequently on an annual basis, the OSO will propose a set of deliverables and dates when major milestones will be delivered. The OSO will be responsible for submitting a performance report to the Workforce Development Board monthly. 

The OSO will report the progress against the scope of work and deliverables to the Workforce Development Board Operations Committee quarterly. The Workforce Development Board will be responsible for monitoring performance levels based on progress reviews and milestone reports. 

The OSO will coordinate Services and staffing of the Job Center as described in the proposed scope of work.

## SECTION 3. DURATION 

The contract will commence upon execution, and remain in effect for four (4) years. 

## SECTION 4. PRICE 

For Services of the on-site lead provided under this MOU, for July 1, 2018, through June 30, 2019, OSO shall receive an award of $116,212.73. For subsequent years, the MOU Price will be amended by the percentage of employee compensation increase approved by the Lake County Board and as budgeted and approved by the Workforce Development Board. 

The OSO partner consortium will carry out its leadership advisory functions associated with the Lake County Job Center One-Stop-Operator proposal at no additional cost.

Workforce Development Board and Lake County reserve the right to terminate this MOU, or any part of this MOU, with or without cause, upon thirty (30) days written notice.
