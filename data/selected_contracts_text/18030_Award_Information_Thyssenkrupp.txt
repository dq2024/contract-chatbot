## **Scope of Work:** 

Thyssenkrupp Elevator (TK) shall perform regularly scheduled maintenance on a routine basis.  TK will examine the elevator equipment to ensure optimum operation in addition to lubricating equipment for smooth and efficient performance. 

## **Procurement Method:** 

Facilities identified a national cooperative contract with the National Cooperative Purchasing Alliance (NCPA) that provides the cost for goods, services and labor at an already negotiated rate.  This is authorized by Article 10, Section 101 of the Lake County Purchasing Ordinance. 

## **Department Recommendation:** 

The Purchasing Division vetted the contract with the National Cooperative Purchasing Alliance (NCPA) http://www.ncpa.us/Vendors/Details/1211 and determined that it met the requirements pursuant to State of Illinois Joint Purchasing Act and the Lake County Purchasing Ordinance.  Lake County may participate in a joint purchasing agreement for the procurement of goods with one or more public procurement units in accordance with an agreement entered into between the participants.
