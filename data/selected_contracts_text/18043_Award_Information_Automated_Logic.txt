## **Scope of Work:** 

Provide routine inspections for of devices such as relays, pressure to electrical switches, electrical to pressure switches, differential pressure switches, pressure to current transducers, current to pressure transducers, pressure to resistance transducers, humidity sensors, and temperature sensors. Systems and Equipment have been installed at the following facilities: Public Defender, Branch Courts, Division of Transportation, Central Permit, and Depke Juvenile Facility. 

## **Procurement Method:** 

Facilities identified the contract with General Services Administration (GSA) that provides the cost for goods, services and labor at an already negotiated rate.  This is authorized by Article 10, Section 101 of the Lake County Purchasing Ordinance. 

## **Department Recommendation:** 

The Purchasing Division vetted the contract with the GSA and determined that it met the requirements pursuant to State of Illinois Joint Purchasing Act and the Lake County Purchasing Ordinance.  Lake County may participate in a joint purchasing agreements for the procurement of goods with one or more public procurement units in accordance with an agreement entered into between the participants.
