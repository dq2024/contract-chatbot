MODIFICATION NO. 1 

Behavioral Health Services for Lake County 

## TO AGREEMENT 22046 

This Modification to Agreement 22046 (“Modification”) is made effective upon signing by Lake County, IL (“County”) and Blain and Associates, PC ("Blain"), with principal offices at PO Box 855, Grayslake, IL 60030. The County and Blain are referred to as the “Parties.” 

## RECITALS 

## WHEREAS, 

1. Blain was awarded a contract by the County to provide Behavioral Health Services for Lake County’s juvenile, adult and emerging adult sex offenders, which was executed on June 21, 2022. 

2. Blain requested an increase for the psychological evaluation fee due to increased expenses incurred to provide the service. 

3. The Parties have agreed to amend the terms of the Agreement as set forth in this Modification. 

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the recitals above and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree that the Agreement shall be modified as follows: 

## Modification of existing sections of the Agreement: 

## SECTION 4. AGREEMENT PRICE 

The County will approve a one-time increase in accordance with the service rates below. The rate increase will be effective for evaluations completed on or after August 1, 2023.
