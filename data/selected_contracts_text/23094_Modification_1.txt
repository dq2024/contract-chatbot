## Overview: 

ReferralGPS, through the ARPA grant allocation of $300,000 for the CareLink program, wiil provide targeted mental health navigation and coordination services to eligible participants in Lake County. This enhanced scope of work outlines the specific deliverables, associated activities, and budget allocations. 

## Scope of Work Components 

1. CareLink Navigation and Triage ReferralGPS will provide CareLink navigation and triage services for students and families seeking mental health support. This includes: 

| 

   - Matching participants with appropriate mental health resources based on 

   - o Initial assessment of mental health needs. severity, preference, and location. 

   - o Ensuring multilingual support where necessary to maximize accessibility. 

2. Provision of Care Funds Allocated funds will directly support the provision of mental health care services for eligible participants. Activities include: 

   - Covering therapy session costs for participants lacking financial resources. 

   - Coordinating payments with partnered mental health providers to ensure seamless delivery of care. 

3. Point of Contact Services ReferralGPS will act as the primary liaison for: 

   - Clinical practices, ensuring alignment with the CareLink program goals. 

   - Families and students, offering consistent communication and updates throughout the care process. 

   - Schools, supporting their role in identifying students who require services and providing ongoing feedback. 

| 

4. Reporting and Data Management ReferralGPS will provide detailed aggregate reporting to monitor program performance, including: 

   - Number of sessions funded and participant demographics. 

   - Metrics on treatment engagement and outcomes. 

   - o Trends and insights to facilitate data-driven decision-making for future 

      - program enhancements.
